# Psychological Safety

- beleaf that an environment is
  - safe for interpersonal risk taking
  - exposing vulnerability
  - contributing perspectives
  - without fear of negative consequences

[@McClintock2023-af]

## Misconceptions about Psychological Safety

- Psychological safety is not about being nice
- Psychological safety is not a personality issue
- Psychological safety is not another name for trust
- Psychological safety does not lower the bar for performance standards

[@Edmondson2018-va]

# Video Playback Speed

## 2x speed and memeory retention

- Medical students
    - 2x speed video playback did not negatively impact memory retention in medical students
        - in immediate recall, and delayed recall [@Kiyak2023-zc]
- Older Adult
    - 2x speed video playback did negatively impact memory retention in older adults [@Murphy2023-tg]

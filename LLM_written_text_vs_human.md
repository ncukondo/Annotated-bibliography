# Comparing AI-Generated and Human-Written Texts

## Researches

### Contrasting Linguistic Patterns in Human and LLM-Generated News Text 

Real news articles and LLM-generated news (from six different models) were compared based on morphology, syntax, sentiment, and bias. Human-written news exhibited greater sentence length variability and richer vocabulary diversity, along with stronger emotional expression. LLM outputs showed less sentence variation, higher usage of numerals and symbols, and an amplification of gender biases present in the training data.

[@Munoz-Ortiz2024-yv]

### Distinguishing Academic Science Writing from Humans or ChatGPT with Over 99% Accuracy Using Off-the-Shelf Machine Learning Tools 

Perspective articles from Science were analyzed to extract features such as paragraph complexity, sentence-length variance, punctuation variety, and technical specificity. A machine learning classifier using these features distinguished human-written texts from ChatGPT (GPT-3.5) outputs with over 99% accuracy.

[@Desaire2023-gy]

### AI-Generated vs Human-Authored Texts: A Multidimensional Comparison

Using Biber’s multidimensional register analysis, the study compared ChatGPT (GPT-3.5) outputs and human-authored texts across registers (conversation, essays, news, academic writing). AI-generated texts were more informational and lacked interpersonal, persuasive, and narrative features. Classification based on these dimensions distinguished AI vs human texts with approximately 79% accuracy for AI-generated and 69% for human texts.

[@Berber-Sardinha2024-to]

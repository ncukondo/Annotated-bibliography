# Small group learning

## Benefits

- Help address gaps in student knowledge
- Encourage self-directed learning
- Allow students to engage with a range of perspectives from their peers
- Allow students to test their ideas and attitudes with their peers
- Promote a willingness for students to share their ideas
- Provide opportunities for students to give and receive feedback
- Help students to develop skills in critical thinking and problem solving
- Help students to develop communication, teamwork and leadership skills.

[@Burgess2020-nz]

## Facilitation Methods

- Various seating arrangements (Figure 3)
- Thinking time
  - Have individuals think and write down their thoughts before starting to talk
- Buzz groups
  - Discuss in small groups
- Snowball groups
  - Start with pairs, then discuss in groups of four, and add questions or complexity if discussing in larger groups
    - Also known as: think-pair-share [@Fakoya2023-is]
- Cross-over groups (Jigsaw)
  - Mix members to form new groups

[@Edmunds2010-ry]

## Unique Methods in Small Group Learning

- Lectures
  - Often overused
- Step-by-step discussions
  - Planned series of tasks or questions
- Free discussions
  - Tutors need to summarize what has been learned
- Brainstorming
- Fishbowl
  - Form a small group in the middle of a larger group for discussion
- Role-playing
  - Good for communication skills training
- Tutor-less groups
  - Share in a whole group session afterward

[@Edmunds2010-ry]
